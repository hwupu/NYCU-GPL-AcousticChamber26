Component preview — buttons, sheets, Main Hall, Debug + presets (round 4)

Proposal for PB-17 (components + motion) and PB-18 (sheets, scrolling, Debug bench, presets). Scheme B (mono + white). Units are canvas units (1 u = 1 pt; at 2 m 1 u = 2 mm ≈ 0.057°). The mouse stands in for the controller ray: hover = ray on the element, click = trigger. Our screens are English; participant screens (Stop dialog, onboarding) follow the participant's language. The participant Language screen is designed later with the onboarding ticket.

1. Buttons and states

Hierarchy by fill. Prominent: the one main action of a screen. Gray: a secondary action that needs a visible target. Borderless: everything else (back, cancel, keys, list actions). Destructive: red fill only on a final confirmation; elsewhere red text. Every state gets brighter: normal → hover → pressed (pressed is the brightest). Red rises in brightness only (same hue and saturation).

3. Sheets: appear in place, push back and dim

The sheet grows from 94 % to full size while it sharpens and fades in, and settles; the window behind scales to 94 % and dims; a second sheet pushes both back. During a trial the sheet appears alone over the dimmed scene. Press Y (keyboard Y works too, Esc closes). The Stop dialog is a participant screen: it follows the participant's language.

4. Main Hall: 2-column grid, borderless scrolling

Large covers (16:9, solid gray placeholders), the C-ID and an "unfinished" pill on the cover, the title below; no description. Cards sit 4 u apart side by side and 8 u apart vertically; each card is a container with 4 u padding, so the hover fill shows 4 u around the cover (concentric corners 40 / 36). No frame, no scrollbar: edges fade only where more content waits; a thin indicator shows while scrolling. Headset: right thumbstick scrolls, or hold the trigger and drag (a click turns into a drag after 12 u).

5. Debug flow with the preset manager

One panel, a stack of pages that change in place (fade + slight scale, no slide). ‹ Back at the top left of every pushed page; × Close at the same spot on sheets you may leave without a choice; decision sheets have Cancel instead. No DEBUG tag on the Debug pages (you know where you are).
Presets follow ADR-0002: Debug edits a working copy; Save overwrites the preset it came from, Save as makes a new one, Make active chooses the preset real sessions use (shown on the chamber menu). "Default" comes from the chamber's Config/default.params and is read-only. Not saved in presets: playback tests (play stimulus, play background) and Debug-only display switches (show audio sphere).
